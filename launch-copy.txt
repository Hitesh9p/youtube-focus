LINKEDIN DRAFT

Opened YouTube for a tutorial. Ended up scrolling instead?

I’m building YouTube Focus, a small Chrome extension to make intentional watching easier.

It hides recommendations and Shorts, lets you hide comments, and reminds you of your task when you start scrolling. You choose the reminder interval.

Search and the video you came for stay available. No account, no AI API, and your settings stay on your device.

Here’s a quick demo 👇

It’s an early version, and I’d love feedback: what distracts you most on YouTube?
