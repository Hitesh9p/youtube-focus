# Privacy policy draft — YouTube Focus

Draft date: October 26, 2026. Before publication, the publisher must add its identity, support contact, effective date, and public policy URL, and verify this policy against the final package.

YouTube Focus operates locally in your browser. It does not collect or transmit personal data, usage analytics, viewing history, search queries, or video details to a developer or third party.

## Local settings

The task text you choose, distraction-hiding and comment-hiding on/off settings, reminder on/off setting, and reminder cooldown in seconds are stored in Chrome’s local extension storage on your device (`chrome.storage.local`, not Chrome sync storage). Settings persist until you change them or uninstall the extension. To clear your task, erase its text and click Save task. Uninstalling the extension removes its local settings through the browser.

## Page access

The extension runs only on `https://www.youtube.com/*` to hide recommendation elements and optional comments, identify the current page type, detect scrolling intent, display reminders, and pause hidden Shorts playback. It does not save page contents or send them anywhere. It does not run on other websites, YouTube embeds on other sites, or mobile YouTube.

## Permissions and third parties

The extension requests the storage permission for settings. It uses no external services, advertising, analytics, cookies API, or remote code. The YouTube website and Chrome browser may process data independently under their own policies; this extension does not prevent that activity.
